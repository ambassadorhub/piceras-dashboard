## How to Use

1. **Add items:** Just mention what you need in this group. I'll add it automatically.
2. **View list:** Ask me "show the list" and I'll pull it up.
3. **Clear list:** Say "clear the list" when you've placed your order.
4. **Add notes:** Mention quantities, brands, or preferences. I'll capture them.

## Format

When you order, I can present this as:
- **Grouped by category** (produce, dairy, pantry, etc.)
- **Simple checklist** (just item names)
- **Full detail** (with notes/brands)

Just tell me which format you prefer when you're ready to order.

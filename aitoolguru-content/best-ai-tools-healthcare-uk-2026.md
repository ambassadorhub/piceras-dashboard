## 1. Birdie

**Best for:** Digital care planning, real-time records, and CQC compliance

**Star rating:** 4.7 / 5

**Starting price:** Approx £35/user/month

### Overview

Birdie replaced the paper care plans that cluttered every domiciliary care office in Britain. A care provider in Leicester uses it to update care records in real time via mobile apps, generate CQC-ready reports automatically, and spot deteriorating health trends before they become emergencies. The AI features analyse visit patterns, flag missed medications, and suggest care plan adjustments based on historical data.

### Key Features

- **Digital care plans:** Create, update, and share care plans in real time. Family members see updates instantly through a secure portal.
- **AI-powered risk alerts:** Machine learning analyses visit data and flags anomalies. A missed visit, a sudden change in vital signs, or a pattern of late arrivals triggers automatic notifications.
- **CQC report generation:** Export inspection-ready reports with one click. No more scrambling to compile paper files the night before an inspection.
- **Medication management:** Track MAR (Medication Administration Records) digitally. Receive alerts for missed doses, refills, and potential interactions.
- **Family app:** Keep relatives informed with photos, updates, and care logs. Reduces phone enquiries by up to 60%.
- **Rostering integration:** Connect Birdie with your scheduling system to ensure the right carer with the right skills attends each visit.
- **Offline mode:** Carers record visits without internet connectivity. Data syncs automatically when signal returns.

### Pros

- CQC inspectors specifically praise Birdie-using providers for record transparency and completeness
- The family app is the best in the market. Relatives report feeling genuinely connected to their loved one's care
- Risk alerts have prevented serious incidents. One provider caught a UTI early because the AI flagged changing toilet visit patterns
- Offline mode means rural areas with poor signal are not disadvantaged
- The onboarding team includes former care managers who understand the sector

### Cons

- At £35/user/month, it is pricier than some competitors. Budget carefully for teams of 50+
- The mobile app can drain battery life on older devices. Carers report needing power banks
- Customisation is limited. Complex care plans with unusual requirements may need workarounds
- Integration with legacy payroll systems is patchy. Expect some manual data entry
- Customer support is excellent during UK business hours but limited overnight

### What Users Say

CQC inspection reports for Birdie users consistently note "good use of technology to support care delivery." A Trustpilot reviewer from a Birmingham domiciliary care agency said Birdie "transformed our inspection outcomes from Requires Improvement to Good in one cycle." Reddit users on r/domiciliarycare praise the family app but complain about occasional app crashes on older Android phones.

### Verdict

**Editor's Choice for domiciliary care planning.** Birdie is the standard against which other care management platforms are measured. If you run a domiciliary care service and still use paper records, this should be your first technology investment.

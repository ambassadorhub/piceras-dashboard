## 1. Workable

**Best for:** End-to-end recruitment for SMEs and growing agencies

**Star rating:** 4.6 / 5

**Starting price:** Approx £99/month

### Overview

Workable is the recruitment platform that scales with your ambition. A London fintech startup used it to grow from 10 to 80 employees in 18 months, managing every role from junior developer to CFO through one system. The AI features write job descriptions, suggest candidates from a database of 400 million profiles, rank applicants by fit, and even predict which candidates will accept your offer.

### Key Features

- **AI job description writer:** Generate optimised job descriptions from a few bullet points. The AI ensures inclusive language, appropriate seniority levels, and SEO-friendly titles.
- **Sourcing AI:** Search 400 million candidate profiles across the web. The AI suggests candidates who match your requirements but might not have applied.
- **Resume parsing and ranking:** Upload 100 CVs and the AI extracts skills, experience, and qualifications, ranking candidates against your criteria in seconds.
- **Interview scheduling:** Automated calendar integration finds mutually available slots across multiple interviewers and time zones.
- **Collaborative hiring:** Scorecards, feedback forms, and structured evaluation criteria ensure every interviewer assesses consistently.
- **Offer management:** Generate offer letters, track acceptance rates, and analyse which offers succeed and why.
- **Reporting and analytics:** Pipeline velocity, source effectiveness, diversity metrics, and cost-per-hire dashboards.

### Pros

- The AI sourcing is genuinely effective. One agency sourced three qualified candidates for a niche DevOps role in 24 hours
- Job description writer produces inclusive, professional copy that attracts diverse applicants
- The collaborative scoring system reduces bias by standardising evaluation criteria
- Integration with 200+ job boards means one-click posting across Indeed, LinkedIn, NHS Jobs, and specialist boards
- Customer support includes live chat with recruiters who understand the hiring process

### Cons

- At £99/month for the Starter plan, it is an investment for agencies placing fewer than 10 roles per month
- The AI ranking occasionally misses context. A candidate with non-linear career progression may score poorly despite being ideal
- Advanced analytics require the Professional plan (£299/month). Starter reporting is basic
- Some users report the mobile app lacks features available on desktop, forcing managers back to their laptops
- Integration with some UK payroll systems requires middleware or manual exports

### What Users Say

G2 reviewers rate Workable 4.5/5, calling it "the most complete recruitment platform for growing companies." A Trustpilot reviewer from a Bristol SaaS company said the AI sourcing "found candidates we would never have discovered through traditional advertising." Reddit users on r/recruitment praise the collaborative features but note the AI job descriptions need human editing to avoid sounding generic.

### Pricing

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | £99/month | 2 active jobs, AI sourcing, basic reporting |
| Professional | £299/month | 20 active jobs, advanced analytics, video interviews |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited jobs, API access, custom integrations, dedicated support |

### Verdict

**Editor's Choice for all-round recruitment.** Workable is the platform we recommend when someone asks "what recruitment software should I buy?" It balances power with usability and scales from your first hire to your hundredth.

## 2. Hiretual (HireEZ)

**Best for:** Talent sourcing, Boolean search, and candidate pipeline building

**Star rating:** 4.5 / 5

**Starting price:** Approx £125/month

### Overview

Hiretual (recently rebranded as HireEZ) is the sourcing engine that powers the best recruitment agencies. A specialist healthcare recruiter in Birmingham uses it to build pipelines of qualified nurses, care workers, and allied health professionals before vacancies even arise. The AI searches across LinkedIn, GitHub, Stack Overflow, NHS staff directories, and dozens of other sources to find candidates who match your precise criteria.

### Key Features

- **AI sourcing assistant:** Describe your ideal candidate in natural language. "Find me a band 6 mental health nurse with CBT training within 50 miles of Manchester" generates a targeted candidate list in seconds.
- **Boolean builder:** Create complex search strings with a visual interface. No need to memorise Boolean operators.
- **Candidate enrichment:** Automatically populate candidate profiles with contact details, skills, and work history from across the web.
- **Diversity sourcing:** AI features that help you build diverse shortlists by expanding your search beyond traditional networks.
- **Pipeline management:** Organise candidates into projects, stages, and talent pools. Nurture relationships before roles open.
- **Engagement automation:** Schedule personalised outreach sequences across email, LinkedIn, and InMail.
- **Market insights:** Analyse talent supply, demand, and salary benchmarks for any role in any location.

### Pros

- The AI sourcing is the most powerful available. Finds candidates that LinkedIn Recruiter misses
- Boolean builder makes complex sourcing accessible to junior recruiters
- Candidate enrichment saves hours of manual profile building
- Diversity sourcing features genuinely expand candidate pools beyond traditional networks
- Market insights help you advise clients on realistic salary expectations and availability

### Cons

- At £125/month, it is a specialist tool. Generalist recruiters may not use its full capability
- The interface is powerful but complex. Expect 2-4 weeks of training before full proficiency
- Contact data accuracy varies by industry. Healthcare and tech are strong; creative industries are weaker
- Engagement automation can feel impersonal if overused. Balance AI efficiency with human touch
- Some users report data freshness issues. Candidates who changed jobs six months ago may still show old employers

### What Users Say

G2 reviewers rate HireEZ 4.4/5, calling it "the sourcing tool that separates good recruiters from great ones." A Trustpilot reviewer from a healthcare staffing agency said the AI sourcing "cut our time-to-fill by 40% for hard-to-fill nursing roles." The main complaint: the rebrand from Hiretual to HireEZ caused some confusion and integration issues that took months to resolve.

### Verdict

## 3. HireVue

**Best for:** Video interviewing, assessment, and candidate experience

**Star rating:** 4.4 / 5

**Starting price:** Custom pricing (typically £5,000-£15,000/year)

### Overview

HireVue transformed the first-round interview from a scheduling nightmare into a scalable assessment process. A national retailer with 200 stores uses it to interview 5,000 seasonal candidates in two weeks, assessing communication skills, problem-solving ability, and cultural fit through structured video responses. The AI analyses verbal responses, facial expressions, and language patterns to identify candidates who match the profiles of top performers.

### Key Features

- **On-demand video interviews:** Candidates record responses at their convenience. Review at 2x speed, share with hiring managers, and compare side-by-side.
- **Live video interviews:** Structured live interviews with integrated assessment tools and real-time scoring.
- **AI-powered assessments:** Game-based assessments that measure cognitive ability, emotional intelligence, and job-specific skills without bias-prone CV screening.
- **Interview analytics:** AI analyses responses for communication clarity, confidence, and content relevance. Identify candidates who interview well but lack substance.
- **Structured evaluation:** Standardised scorecards ensure every candidate is assessed against the same criteria. Reduce unconscious bias.
- **Candidate experience:** Mobile-friendly interface with practice questions and clear instructions. First impressions matter.
- **Integration:** Connects with Workable, Greenhouse, SAP SuccessFactors, and most major ATS platforms.

### Pros

- On-demand interviews eliminate scheduling conflicts. One recruiter can assess 50 candidates in a day
- The AI assessments reduce reliance on CVs, which disproportionately disadvantage non-traditional candidates
- Structured scoring reduces bias. One client increased diversity hires by 35% after implementing HireVue
- The candidate experience is polished. Applicants receive feedback and status updates automatically
- Game-based assessments are engaging. Completion rates exceed 90% compared to 60% for traditional aptitude tests

### Cons

- Custom pricing starts around £5,000/year, excluding small agencies and single-site employers
- Some candidates find video interviews impersonal. The human touch of a phone screen is lost
- AI assessment of facial expressions has faced ethical scrutiny. Ensure transparency with candidates
- Technical requirements (webcam, stable internet) exclude some candidates from disadvantaged backgrounds
- The interface can feel corporate and cold. Brand customisation is limited on lower tiers

### What Users Say

G2 reviewers rate HireVue 4.3/5 for enterprise recruitment. A Trustpilot reviewer from a retail chain said the on-demand interviews "reduced our time-to-hire from 6 weeks to 10 days." However, several candidates on Glassdoor complained that the AI assessment feels "robotic and dehumanising." Ethical concerns about AI evaluating facial expressions remain prominent in recruitment industry discussions.

| Custom | Variable | Volume pricing for high-volume seasonal recruitment |

### Verdict

**Editor's Choice for high-volume and graduate recruitment.** HireVue is a significant investment that pays off for organisations hiring at scale.
